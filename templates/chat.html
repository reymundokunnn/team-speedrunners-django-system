Chat Rules & Guidelines

🎯 Professional Communication

  • Maintain a professional tone in all conversations
  • Use clear and respectful language
  • Avoid slang or informal abbreviations that may be misunderstood
  • Be punctual in your responses when discussing project timelines

🤝 Respect & Collaboration

  • Treat all users with respect regardless of their role
  • Listen actively and acknowledge others' viewpoints
  • Provide constructive feedback when discussing work
  • Celebrate collaborative successes

🔒 Privacy & Security

  • Never share sensitive personal information
  • Do not discuss confidential project details publicly
  • Report any suspicious behavior to administrators
  • Use the platform's reporting features for inappropriate content

🚫 Prohibited Content

  • No harassment, bullying, or discriminatory language
  • No spam, promotional content, or unsolicited advertising
  • No sharing of illegal or copyrighted materials
  • No offensive, violent, or inappropriate content

⚖️ Consequences

Violations of these rules may result in:

  • Warnings from administrators
  • Temporary suspension of chat privileges
  • Permanent account restrictions in severe cases
  • Legal action for serious violations

Remember: This platform exists to foster positive professional relationships. Let's keep our community respectful and productive!
